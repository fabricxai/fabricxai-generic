FINDINGS

1. [Critical (zero tolerance)] (p.4)
   Two of the four fire exits on the 3rd floor sewing area were obstructed by stacked carton pallets at the time of the walk; exit 3B was locked and the key was held in the security office.

2. [Major] (p.5)
   Overtime records for June and July 2026 show 68 operators exceeding the statutory 2 hours per day on 9 and 11 days respectively, without the written consent required by the Bangladesh Labour Rules 2015.

3. [Major] (p.5)
   Chemical store for spot-cleaning solvents has no secondary containment and no MSDS displayed in Bangla; three containers were unlabelled.

4. [Major] (p.6)
   No functioning anti-harassment committee could be evidenced; the last recorded meeting minute is dated 2025-11-04.

5. [Minor] (p.6)
   Drinking-water points on the ground floor were tested at 1 point per 84 workers against the required 1 per 50.

6. [Minor] (p.7)
   Personal protective equipment for the cutting section (metal mesh gloves) was available but not worn by 4 of 11 cutters observed.

7. [Minor] (p.7)
   Machine guards were missing on 2 of 14 button-attach machines inspected.

8. [Observation] (p.8)
   Childcare room is operating above its registered capacity on morning shifts; the factory has applied for an extension.
